## How to play 2048

The board is a 4 × 4 grid. Each move slides **every tile** as far as it can go in one direction. When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one tile showing their sum: two 2s make a 4, two 4s make an 8, and so on. After every move a new tile, a 2 or occasionally a 4, appears in an empty square.

Reach a tile showing **2048** and you have won. You can keep playing for a bigger tile; the game ends when the board is full and no move can merge anything.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Mouse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slide tiles | Arrow keys, or <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> | Swipe on the board | Click and drag on the board |
| Take back one move | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo button | Undo button |

Undo takes back only your last move, so it fixes a slip but cannot be used to replay a whole game. The game in progress and your best score are saved in this browser.

## How scoring works

Every merge adds the value of the new tile to your score. Joining two 512s into 1024 is worth 1,024 points. Spawned tiles earn nothing. Reaching 2048 usually takes about a thousand moves and scores around 20,000 points; the score therefore measures how far you got, not how fast.

## The corner strategy

Almost every strong player uses some version of the same plan:

1. **Choose a corner** for your largest tile, for example the bottom left, and keep it there for the whole game.
2. **Use only three directions** most of the time: for a bottom-left corner, left, down and right. Moving up pulls the big tile out of its corner and lets a small tile drop in behind it.
3. **Keep the bottom row full.** A full row cannot shift sideways, so your big tiles stay put when you press left or right.
4. **Build a descending chain** along the bottom row and then up the next row in a snake: 1024, 512, 256, 128 along the bottom, then 64 and 32 above the 128. Each merge can then cascade along the chain towards the corner.
5. **When you must press the fourth direction,** do it once and immediately press the opposite direction to repair the corner.

## Common mistakes

- **Chasing merges anywhere on the board.** A merge in the wrong place can break your chain. Ask whether it helps the corner before taking it.
- **Letting small tiles get trapped** between large ones. A lone 2 stuck beside a 256 can block the chain for dozens of moves.
- **Pressing keys quickly in a fixed rhythm.** Patterns like "down, left, down, left" work early on and fail badly in the mid-game, when every move needs thought.

## A little history

2048 was written by Italian developer Gabriele Cirulli and released in March 2014 as an open-source web game. It was inspired by the earlier games *1024* and *Threes!*, and became one of the most played browser games of that year. The largest tile that is possible on a 4 × 4 board is 131,072, which needs every square filled with a different power of two at the right moment; few players ever see 8192.
