What you get

  • Proper rules. Every Sudoku has exactly one solution. Minesweeper's first click is always safe and uses the classic board sizes. Solitaire is standard Klondike with Draw 1 and Draw 3.
  • Works on a phone. Touch targets are sized for fingers: a number pad for Sudoku, long-press and a flag mode for Minesweeper, tap-to-move and drag for Solitaire.
  • Keyboard support. Arrow keys and number keys in Sudoku, arrow keys and F in Minesweeper, Ctrl+Z to undo.
  • Saved on your device. Progress and best times are kept in your browser's local storage. Nothing about your play is sent to a server.
  • A guide under every game. Each page explains the rules and the techniques that actually improve your results.

About these games

Guagua Games is a small independent project by Alvin Li, who also maintains the developer tools at guagua-alvin.top. The games are written from scratch in plain JavaScript with no game engine, which is why each page is only a few tens of kilobytes. More games will be added one at a time, when each is finished properly.
