What you get

  • Proper rules. Every Sudoku has exactly one solution. Minesweeper's first click is always safe. FreeCell uses the classic numbered deals. Klondike, Spider and Pyramid follow the standard rules.
  • Sudoku on paper too. Printable Sudoku makes sheets of one to six puzzles with answer pages.
  • Works on a phone. Touch targets are sized for fingers: a number pad for Sudoku, long-press and a flag mode for Minesweeper, tap-to-move and drag for the card games.
  • Keyboard support. Arrow keys and number keys in Sudoku, arrow keys and F in Minesweeper, Ctrl+Z to undo everywhere.
  • Saved on your device. Progress, streaks and best times are kept in your browser's local storage. Nothing about your play is sent to a server.
  • A guide under every game. Each page explains the rules and the techniques that actually improve your results.

About these games

Guagua Games is a small independent project by Alvin Li, who also maintains the developer tools at guagua-alvin.top. The games are written from scratch in plain JavaScript with no game engine, so each page loads quickly even on a slow connection. New games are added one at a time, when each is finished properly.
