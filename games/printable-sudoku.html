Printable Sudoku

Choose a level and layout, check the preview, and print. New puzzles every time, with answer pages if you want them.

How to print Sudoku puzzles

  1. Choose a difficulty, how many puzzles per page, and how many pages you want.
  2. The preview above shows exactly what will print. Each change generates a fresh set of puzzles.
  3. Press Print and use your browser's print dialog. Choose your paper size (A4 or US Letter both work) and, if your browser offers it, "Fit to page" or a scale of 100%.
  4. Tick Include answers to add answer pages after the puzzles. Each answer grid shows the givens in bold and the solution digits in grey, with the same puzzle number, so you can check one puzzle without seeing the others.

Everything is generated in your browser. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic, without guessing.

For hard and expert puzzles, choose one or two per page. Those levels need pencil candidates, and small grids get crowded quickly.

Solving on paper

Paper Sudoku is a different experience from playing on a screen. There is no mistake counter and nothing fills in notes for you, so a few habits help:

  • Use a pencil and a good eraser. Even careful solvers erase.
  • Write candidates small. Two common styles exist. Corner marks put a digit in a corner of the cell when it can only be in two places in that box. Centre marks list every possible digit in the middle of the cell. Many solvers use corners early and centre marks on hard puzzles.
  • Mark digits as you finish them. Writing 1–9 along the margin and crossing off each digit once all nine are placed shows at a glance what is left.
  • Check rows as you go. On paper, a mistake is found late and costs a lot. Glance along the row, column and box each time you write a digit in ink.

For teachers and clubs

Sudoku is a popular logic exercise in classrooms, libraries and activity groups because it needs no arithmetic and the rules fit in one sentence. You are welcome to print and photocopy these puzzles for personal use, classrooms, care homes and clubs. Print a set at one difficulty with answers for a worksheet, or mix levels by printing pages separately.

Puzzle numbers

Each puzzle carries a six-digit number. It identifies the puzzle on the answer pages and makes it easy to refer to a particular grid when you hand out several sheets. Want to play on screen instead? The online Sudoku has notes, hints and undo, and there is a new daily puzzle every day.
