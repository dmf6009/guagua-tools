What makes a Sudoku easy

Easy puzzles start with around 40 of the 81 cells filled in. With that much information, there is almost always a cell somewhere that can be completed by simple scanning, so you can solve from start to finish without writing down any candidates. That makes easy Sudoku the right place to learn the game, and a good warm-up for experienced players who want to work on speed.

Every puzzle here has exactly one solution and is generated in your browser when you load the page or press New puzzle, so you will not run out.

The two techniques that solve every easy puzzle

Cross-hatching (hidden singles). Pick a digit, say 7, and a 3×3 box that does not have a 7 yet. Look along the three rows and three columns that run through that box. Every 7 you see rules out the whole row or column inside the box. If only one empty cell remains, the 7 goes there. Work through the digits 1 to 9 in turn, and through every box.

Last cell standing (naked singles). Pick an empty cell and check its row, column and box. If eight of the nine digits already appear among them, the ninth is the answer. This is quickest for cells in crowded areas of the grid.

Most players alternate: cross-hatch a digit across the whole grid, then look for crowded cells, then go back to cross-hatching.

A routine for beginners

  1. Start with the digit that already appears most often. It has the fewest places left, so it is the easiest to finish.
  2. Complete that digit everywhere you can before moving to the next.
  3. When a row, column or box has only one or two gaps, fill those next. The number pad shows how many of each digit are still missing.
  4. If you have looked at every digit and every crowded cell without progress, switch on Notes and write candidates in the cells of one busy box. The answer usually appears immediately.

Typical times

Times vary with the puzzle and with whether you use a mouse, a keyboard or a touch screen. The keyboard is fastest: arrow keys to move, number keys to enter.

When to move up

If you can finish easy puzzles in under ten minutes without hints, try medium Sudoku. The techniques are the same at first, but you will start to need pencil notes, and the puzzles reward patience rather than speed.

Frequently asked questions

How many givens does an easy Sudoku have?

About 40 of the 81 cells. Difficulty is set by removing givens while keeping exactly one solution.

Does every puzzle have only one solution?

Yes. Each puzzle is generated in your browser and checked by a solver so that exactly one solution exists. You never need to guess.

Is my progress saved?

The current puzzle, your notes and the timer are saved in this browser's local storage, so you can close the page and continue later. Nothing is sent to a server.
