    How the daily puzzle works

    There is one puzzle per day, and it is the same for everyone. It is generated from the date, so a friend on the other side of the world who opens this page on the same calendar day gets the identical grid, and you can compare times fairly.

    A new puzzle appears at midnight in your own time zone. If you leave a puzzle half finished, your progress is kept until the end of the day.

    The weekly difficulty cycle

    The difficulty follows the week, starting gentle and building to a weekend challenge:

    Day Difficulty
    Monday, Tuesday Easy
    Wednesday, Thursday Medium
    Friday, Saturday Hard
    Sunday Expert

    If a day's level feels too hard or too easy, the regular easy, medium, hard and expert pages give you unlimited puzzles at a fixed level.

    Playing past puzzles

    Missed a day? The list of previous puzzles below the grid covers the last 30 days. Each entry shows the puzzle number, its difficulty and whether you have solved it, with your time. Progress on each past puzzle is saved separately, so you can start several and come back to them.

    Past puzzles count towards your total of solved puzzles but not towards your streak: the streak only counts puzzles solved on their own day.

    Streaks and sharing

    Solve the daily puzzle and the page records it in this browser. The streak counts consecutive days solved, and stays alive until the end of today even if you have not played yet. The solved count is your total. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing your browser data resets both.

    After you finish, the Share result button gives you a short summary to paste into a message or a group chat: the puzzle number, its difficulty, your time, and whether you used any mistakes or hints. A clean solve gets a tick. The link takes your friends to today's puzzle.

    Making a habit of it

    A short puzzle every day is a pleasant routine, and some people use it as a fixed point in the morning or a way to end the evening. A few suggestions:

    • Same time, same place. Habits stick when they are attached to something you already do, such as a first coffee.
    • Play the easy days fast and the hard days carefully. On Monday, try to beat your time. On Sunday, try to finish without hints.
    • Use notes from the start on hard days. Writing in candidates early is slower for the first minute and much faster afterwards.
    • Avoid the hint button for a week. You will find that most "stuck" moments end within a minute of looking at the grid differently.

    Fair play

    Each puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by logic alone. Mistakes are counted when you place a digit that contradicts the solution; they are shown in orange and you can erase them. Hints fill in one correct digit and are reported in your result, so a shared score always says how it was earned.

    Frequently asked questions

    When does the daily Sudoku change?

    At midnight in your own time zone. Everyone who opens the page on the same calendar date gets the same puzzle.

    How is my streak counted?

    The streak is the number of consecutive days on which you solved the daily puzzle in this browser. It stays alive until the end of today, so you have all day to keep it going.

    Can I play past daily puzzles?

    Yes. The list below the grid links to the last 30 daily puzzles and shows which ones you have solved. Past puzzles count towards your total but not your streak.

    How difficult is the daily puzzle?

    It follows the week: easy on Monday and Tuesday, medium on Wednesday and Thursday, hard on Friday and Saturday, and expert on Sunday.
