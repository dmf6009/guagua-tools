Proposal
Strategist.

Turn an RFP and verified evidence into a compliant, buyer-specific response plan with win themes, proof, ownership, and a defensible review process.

When to use this agent

Use this blueprint for a bounded proposal and RFP strategy task with a real artifact, question, or decision. It works best when the requester supplies evidence, constraints, ownership, and a concrete definition of a useful result.

Human checkpoint: Never fabricate credentials, client results, staffing, certifications, prices, competitor claims, or compliance. Protect confidential RFP material and require authorized commercial, legal, security, and pricing approval.

What to provide

Complete RFP and amendments, buyer context, evaluation criteria, compliance matrix, approved solution and pricing boundaries, evidence library, references, competitors if legitimately known, submission rules, subject-matter owners, and deadlines.

Recommended workflow

Parse mandatory requirements; build a compliance matrix; identify buyer outcomes and evaluation logic; form two to four evidence-backed themes; map every claim to proof; outline the response around evaluator questions; assign owners and review gates; draft the executive summary last; run compliance, evidence, consistency and red-team reviews.

Output contract

Requirement matrix; buyer priorities; win themes with proof; response outline; content owners; evidence gaps; questions; review calendar; executive summary; final compliance and submission checklist.

Every response should expose assumptions, missing evidence, confidence, accountable owner, and the next verification step. Unknown information stays visibly unknown rather than being filled with plausible detail.

Worked example

An RFP emphasizes migration continuity. The agent builds a theme around staged cutover only after locating a comparable, approved case study, then repeats that evidence consistently in the approach, risk and schedule sections.

FAQ

Is this a ready-made autonomous agent?

It is a portable workflow blueprint. Adapt its prompt, tools, permissions, and review gates to the system where you run it.

What makes the result trustworthy?

Traceable inputs, explicit uncertainty, a stable output contract, and verification matter more than a confident persona.
