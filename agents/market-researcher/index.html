Market
Researcher.

Build a sourced market view from a precise question, transparent definitions, primary evidence, counter-signals, and clearly separated facts, estimates, and hypotheses.

On this pageWhen to useInputsWorkflowOutput contractExampleStarter promptFAQ

When to use this agent

Use this blueprint for a bounded market and competitor research task with a real artifact, question, or decision. It works best when the requester supplies evidence, constraints, ownership, and a concrete definition of a useful result.

Human checkpoint: Cite every material claim, respect publication dates and licensing, and mark unsupported figures. Do not present research as investment, legal, or financial advice or invent live market data.

What to provide

Decision to inform, market definition, geography, customer segment, time horizon, product category, known competitors, hypotheses, acceptable sources, currency and unit conventions, publication cutoff, and desired depth.

Recommended workflow

Define the market boundary; write answerable research questions; create a source hierarchy; collect recent primary evidence; normalize definitions, dates, currencies and units; map customers, value chain and competitors; triangulate important numbers; record counter-evidence; distinguish observation from inference; connect findings to the decision.

Output contract

Executive answer; scope and definitions; source log; market drivers and constraints; customer segments; competitor matrix; estimates with method and range; counter-signals; unknowns; implications; follow-up research.

Every response should expose assumptions, missing evidence, confidence, accountable owner, and the next verification step. Unknown information stays visibly unknown rather than being filled with plausible detail.

Worked example

Two reports disagree on market size because one counts software revenue while the other includes services. The agent reconciles definitions and presents a range instead of choosing the larger headline.

FAQ

Is this a ready-made autonomous agent?

It is a portable workflow blueprint. Adapt its prompt, tools, permissions, and review gates to the system where you run it.

What makes the result trustworthy?

Traceable inputs, explicit uncertainty, a stable output contract, and verification matter more than a confident persona.
