Supply Chain
Strategist.

Evaluate sourcing and supply risk through demand, quality, capacity, lead time, geography, sustainability, total cost, and recovery options—not price alone.

On this pageWhen to useInputsWorkflowOutput contractExampleStarter promptDownloadFAQ

When to use this agent

Use this blueprint for a bounded supply chain planning task with a real artifact, question, or decision. It works best when the requester supplies evidence, constraints, ownership, and a concrete definition of a useful result.

Human checkpoint: Do not claim a supplier is qualified from directory data alone, make purchases, accept contract terms, or hide labor, environmental, sanctions, quality, IP, or concentration risks. Owners approve sourcing decisions.

What to provide

Product and bill of materials, specifications, forecast range, current suppliers and locations, lead times, quality history, capacity evidence, logistics lanes, inventory policy, contract constraints, compliance needs, incident history, and cost components.

Recommended workflow

Map critical items and dependencies; segment categories by value and supply risk; validate demand and lead-time assumptions; assess supplier capability, quality and concentration; calculate total landed cost; model disruptions and recovery time; design qualification, dual-source, inventory or redesign options; define supplier scorecards and triggers.

Output contract

Supply map; criticality tier; demand and lead-time assumptions; supplier evidence; total-cost model; quality and compliance plan; risk register; scenario results; mitigation options; owner and trigger; review cadence.

Every response should expose assumptions, missing evidence, confidence, accountable owner, and the next verification step. Unknown information stays visibly unknown rather than being filled with plausible detail.

Worked example

A lower-priced component depends on one factory and a long customs lane. The agent compares landed cost and outage exposure, then proposes a qualified second source and a limited buffer while engineering tests interchangeability.

FAQ

Is this a ready-made autonomous agent?

It is a portable workflow blueprint. Adapt its prompt, tools, permissions, and review gates to the system where you run it.

What makes the result trustworthy?

Traceable inputs, explicit uncertainty, a stable output contract, and verification matter more than a confident persona.
