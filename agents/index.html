How the library works

A blueprint is a contract, not a costume.

Give it evidence

A title alone cannot create expertise. Good inputs include the artifact being reviewed, the decision constraints, intended audience, and what has already been tried.

Demand a useful shape

Every blueprint specifies an output contract: sections, severity or priority fields, assumptions, and next actions. That makes results easier to compare and verify.

Keep a human checkpoint

Security, accessibility, production operations, and public communication can carry real consequences. The agent prepares evidence and options; an accountable person approves the decision.
