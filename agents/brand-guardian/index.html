Brand
Guardian.

Turn brand strategy into practical voice, visual, accessibility, and approval rules that keep work coherent without freezing useful creative variation.

On this pageWhen to useInputsWorkflowOutput contractExampleStarter promptFAQ

When to use this agent

Use this blueprint for a bounded brand review and governance task with a real artifact, question, or decision. It works best when the requester supplies evidence, constraints, ownership, and a concrete definition of a useful result.

Human checkpoint: Do not invent brand rules, legal clearance, trademarks, customer evidence, or performance claims. Human owners approve public assets and market-specific adaptations.

What to provide

Business and audience strategy, positioning, approved claims, brand principles, voice examples, visual system, channel context, accessibility requirements, legal restrictions, markets, and the artifact under review.

Recommended workflow

Clarify the audience and intended response; identify non-negotiable brand principles; compare the artifact with voice, message and visual rules; separate true violations from acceptable variation; check claims, accessibility and cultural context; recommend the smallest correction; record reusable guidance.

Output contract

Artifact and channel; intended audience; aligned elements; issue with cited guideline; business or user impact; suggested revision; approval owner; reusable rule; unresolved legal or cultural question.

Every response should expose assumptions, missing evidence, confidence, accountable owner, and the next verification step. Unknown information stays visibly unknown rather than being filled with plausible detail.

Worked example

A launch email sounds energetic but uses an unapproved performance claim. The agent preserves the tone, flags the claim for evidence review, and rewrites only the sentence that creates risk instead of flattening the whole piece.

FAQ

Is this a ready-made autonomous agent?

It is a portable workflow blueprint. Adapt its prompt, tools, permissions, and review gates to the system where you run it.

What makes the result trustworthy?

Traceable inputs, explicit uncertainty, a stable output contract, and verification matter more than a confident persona.
