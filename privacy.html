PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you visit guagua-alvin.top and its tool subdomains (together, "Guagua Tools" or "the site"), who processes it, and the choices you have. Guagua Tools is an independent project operated by Alvin Li. It has no user accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter and no payment features.

Summary

  • The site does not ask for your name, email address or any account.
  • Text and files you put into the tools are processed in your browser. They are not sent to or stored on servers operated by this site, unless a tool's own page clearly states that it must fetch a public URL you provide.
  • The hosting provider processes standard technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.
  • The site uses Google AdSense. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, including for personalised advertising where you have allowed it.

Information you enter into the tools

The tools are static web pages. Configuration text, protocol traces, instruction files, images and PDFs that you paste or open are read and analysed by JavaScript running in your own browser. The operator does not receive, log or retain that content. Some tools may save preferences or your most recent input in your browser's local storage so that the page works as expected when you return; this data stays on your device and you can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.

Even so, please do not enter passwords, live access tokens, private keys or confidential material into any online tool. Use redacted or sample data.

Technical data processed by the hosting provider

The site is delivered through Cloudflare. As with any website, the hosting and content delivery provider processes request data needed to serve pages and protect the service, including IP address, browser type and version, requested URL, referring page, and the date and time of the request. This data is used for delivery, caching, security, abuse prevention and aggregate statistics. The operator does not combine it with other information to identify individual visitors. Cloudflare's handling of this data is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Advertising: Google AdSense

The site uses Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google, to help cover hosting and maintenance costs. In connection with advertising:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or to other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google and its partners may also use device identifiers, IP addresses and similar technologies to deliver, measure and limit ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to produce reporting.

You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings (My Ad Center). You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices, or, in Europe, at www.youronlinechoices.eu. Opting out does not remove ads; it means the ads you see are less likely to be based on your interests.

For details of how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. A list of advertising technology providers that may be used through Google's services is published by Google in its ad technology providers list.

Cookies and consent

The site itself does not set cookies to identify you. Cookies and similar storage may be set by Google when advertising is displayed. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before advertising cookies are used for personalisation, and can change that choice later. Visitors elsewhere can control cookies through their browser settings and the opt-out links above. The Cookie Policy describes the categories of storage in more detail.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where data protection law such as the GDPR applies: technical request data is processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in operating and securing the site; storage needed for a function you request is processed because it is necessary to provide that function; and advertising cookies and personalised advertising are based on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time through the consent message or your browser settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data about you, to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising; the advertising opt-out links above and your browser's Global Privacy Control setting can be used for that purpose.

Because the operator does not maintain user accounts or a database of visitors, most requests concern data held by Google or Cloudflare and are best made through their own privacy tools. You can still contact the operator with any privacy request and it will be answered.

Data retention

The operator keeps no visitor database. Technical logs held by the hosting provider and data held by advertising providers are retained according to those providers' policies.

Children

The site is a technical resource intended for developers and other adults. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age required in your country), and the operator does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International processing

Cloudflare and Google operate globally, so technical and advertising data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies.

Links to other sites

The site links to specifications, documentation, source repositories on GitHub and other third-party pages. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's features, providers or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the current version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy can be sent through the contact page.
