How these guides are written

The guides are written by the maintainer of the tools on this site, while building and testing those tools against real client configurations and the published protocol documents. They are not generated from keyword lists and they do not republish vendor documentation: where a rule comes from a specification, the page links to it; where something is a practical recommendation rather than a requirement, the page says so.

The Model Context Protocol changes quickly. Each guide shows its last review date, and corrections are welcome through the contact page. The testing method explains how examples are checked and what a passing check does and does not mean.
